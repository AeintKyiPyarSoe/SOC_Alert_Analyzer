How Priority Scoring Works (0–100 Demo Policy)

The 0–100 score indicates triage precedence rather than a statistical probability of compromise or vendor risk score.

MITRE ATT&CK® Mapping Limitations

Technique mappings are extracted directly from detection metadata provided in the telemetry record. They are explicitly tagged "From event · unverified" because detection of a technique signature does not constitute confirmed compromise.

Privacy & Session Behavior

Review marks and checklist step completions are persisted safely in the browser tab's sessionStorage. The prototype operates locally without uploading imported alert contents to third-party AI models or tracking telemetry.
